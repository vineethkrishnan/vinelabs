name: WeSpend
tagline: Household expense tracker for shared monthly budgets
description: Expo + React Native app for couples and families who share a monthly pot. One person funds the budget, everyone logs spending against automatic daily and weekly limits from their own phone, and at the end of each week the app works out who needs reimbursing. Real-time sync via Supabase with an offline cache.
language: TypeScript
license: MIT
status: beta
categories:
  - mobile-apps
highlights:
  - Shared monthly pot with an automatic daily limit and pro-rated weekly limits
  - Rollover that matches real life - unspent weekly budget carries forward
  - One-tap weekly settlement that refunds own-pocket spends per member
  - Real-time multi-device sync with an offline cache
  - Invite-code onboarding with roles and a single finance manager
order: 60
